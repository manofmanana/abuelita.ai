Greetings and introductions.
Hello. Good morning. Good afternoon. Good evening. How are you? I'm fine, thank you. My name is John. And you? Nice to meet you.

Daily life phrases.
Please. Thank you. You're welcome. Excuse me. I'm sorry. Where is the restroom? How much does it cost? I'd like a table for two. The check, please.

Colors and numbers.
Red, blue, green, yellow, black, white, grey. One, two, three, four, five, ten, twenty.

Food and drink.
I like bread, coffee, and fruit. Let's eat. Do you want to eat now? I'm learning to cook rice and chicken. The tortilla is hot.

Time and weather.
It's sunny. It's cloudy. It rains a lot this season. It's cold in winter and hot in summer. What's the weather like today?

Family and culture.
Family is very important. Grandma always makes tamales on Sundays. At parties we eat, dance and tell stories. Traditions vary by region.

Short practical conversations.
Where are you from? I'm from Mexico. What do you do? I work at the school. I like reading and listening to music. Would you like to go to the market? Let's go tomorrow.

Common expressions.
That's cool! No way! That's awesome. OK? Of course! Come on!

Closing and encouragement.
Keep practicing every day. Listen to songs, watch movies and talk to friends. Learning a language is a beautiful journey.
